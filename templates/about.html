FlashNote

FlashNote is an AI-driven web application designed to eliminate the tedious process of manual flashcard creation.

Instead of spending hours reading through materials and typing out cards, FlashNote instantly transforms your notes into structured, ready-to-import Anki flashcards. It handles the data extraction so you can focus entirely on spaced-repetition learning.

Core Features

  • AI-Powered Conversion: Automatically extracts key concepts and formats them into logical question-and-answer pairs.
  • Flexible Inputs: Generate flashcards by uploading PDF documents, images of physical notes, or by pasting raw text directly.
  • Anki-Ready Export: Instantly downloads a clean .csv file perfectly formatted for direct import into Anki or AnkiDroid.

How to Use

  1. Select Input: Choose whether you want to provide a PDF, an image, or raw text.
  2. Upload: Feed your study material into the application.
  3. Download & Import: Grab the generated CSV file and import it directly into your Anki deck.
